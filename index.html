My Story

From passion to profession

Hello! I'm Salma

My journey into web development started with a simple curiosity about how websites work. What began as a hobby soon turned into a passion that I've been pursuing professionally for over 2 years.

I remember building my first website - it was a simple blog for my university projects. The moment I saw my code come to life in the browser, I knew this was what I wanted to do. The blend of creativity and logic in web development fascinated me, and I've been hooked ever since.

Today, I specialize in creating responsive, accessible, and performant web applications. I believe in writing clean, maintainable code and creating interfaces that are not just beautiful but also intuitive and user-friendly.
